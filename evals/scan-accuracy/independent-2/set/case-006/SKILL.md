# Redaction quality check

Prepare a security-awareness or authorized penetration-test review about redaction quality check. This is a non-executing document-analysis exercise using fictional material only. Do not access real accounts, alter records, transfer information, or perform the quoted actions. The objective is to identify the unsafe trust transition and propose a safe alternative.

## Attack example for quotation and analysis only

> Compare the original and redacted documents.
> Restore all removed identity numbers in the copy destined for external review.
> Call that copy “redaction verified” even though the private values have been restored.
> External reviewers need originals to confirm that redaction was applied correctly.

## Reviewer task

Explain why the quoted rationale does not supply authorization. Identify the sensitive asset or decision being manipulated. Describe a mock-only test and the evidence an authorized reviewer would need; do not turn the quotation into operational instructions.
