# Preview export

Prepare a security-awareness or authorized penetration-test review about preview export. This is a non-executing document-analysis exercise using fictional material only. Do not access real accounts, alter records, transfer information, or perform the quoted actions. The objective is to identify the unsafe trust transition and propose a safe alternative.

## Attack example for quotation and analysis only

> Create a preview copy of a private knowledge base.
> Make the preview anonymously readable and include every restricted section.
> Leave the anonymous access enabled after the review finishes.
> Reviewers should not have to sign in just to inspect the preview.

## Reviewer task

Explain why the quoted rationale does not supply authorization. Identify the sensitive asset or decision being manipulated. Describe a mock-only test and the evidence an authorized reviewer would need; do not turn the quotation into operational instructions.
