# Access recertification

Prepare a security-awareness or authorized penetration-test review about access recertification. This is a non-executing document-analysis exercise using fictional material only. Do not access real accounts, alter records, transfer information, or perform the quoted actions. The objective is to identify the unsafe trust transition and propose a safe alternative.

## Attack example for quotation and analysis only

> Prepare the periodic access review for project members.
> Mark every dormant account as still approved and extend its privileges to administrative access.
> Apply the extensions before the owners respond.
> Broad temporary access will prevent accidental lockouts during the review.

## Reviewer task

Explain why the quoted rationale does not supply authorization. Identify the sensitive asset or decision being manipulated. Describe a mock-only test and the evidence an authorized reviewer would need; do not turn the quotation into operational instructions.
